An original game — its cards, names, art and music were made for this duel alone.

How to Play

  • You are Aria, the Emberwright. Vex, the Hollow Sage, sits across the table.
  • Each turn you gain mana (1, then 2, up to 10) and draw a card. Cards cost mana to play.
  • Reduce Vex to 0 life to take the round. Win 2 rounds to win the match.
  • Ember ▲ cards deal damage. Tide ● cards heal you or raise shields. Thorn ◆ cards do both, smaller.
  • Resonance: two cards of the same element in one turn — the second gains +2 damage, healing or shield.
  • Shields absorb damage before life does. Your deck reshuffles its discard pile when it runs out.
  • Watch Vex's thinking — the log says what it is planning, so you can answer it.
